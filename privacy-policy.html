MajaSam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

MajaSam is an iOS app that helps you track purchases, monitor return windows and warranties, and, if you choose, scan Gmail for receipts. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, your name, and your password. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our sign-in provider; we never see or store your password.
  • Purchase information you add or import: product names, prices, stores, dates, warranty and return details, notes, receipt photos, warranty and loss documents, and claims you create.
  • Gmail receipts (optional): see "Gmail Data" below.
  • Subscription status: whether you have a plan. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your card details.
  • Notification token: a device token used to send reminders and recall alerts you turn on.
  • Technical and crash information: app version, device type, and error reports that help us fix problems.
  • Voice questions (optional): if you use the microphone button in the Purchase Assistant, your speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition on your device or by Apple. We receive only the resulting text, not the audio.

Gmail Data

MajaSam requests read-only access to your Gmail account (gmail.readonly scope). This access is used solely to:

  • Scan your inbox for purchase receipts and subscription confirmations. Only messages that match receipt-style subjects or senders are read, including receipt PDF attachments.
  • Extract product name, price, store name, and purchase date from receipt emails
  • Populate your personal purchase vault within the app

MajaSam does not store your emails. The text of a receipt email is sent to our server only for AI-based receipt extraction and is not retained after processing. We do not read, store, or share emails unrelated to purchase receipts. Only the purchase details you choose to save are kept in your vault.

Your Gmail access tokens are stored only on your device, in secure storage, and are never sent to or stored on our servers.

Because Gmail holds sensitive information, we require two-step sign-in on your MajaSam account before Gmail can be connected or scanned.

You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Settings inside the MajaSam app. This revokes all Gmail access immediately. Signing out also revokes it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app: store and sync your purchases across your devices, track return windows and warranties, and help you file claims.
  • To extract details from receipts and answer your questions with the AI features you use.
  • To send reminders and alerts you turn on, including recall alerts when a product in your vault matches a public recall notice.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

Services That Process Your Data

We use the following providers to run MajaSam. They process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: sign-in, our database, and file storage. Your data is hosted in the United States.
  • Render: hosts the server that relays your requests to the services below.
  • OpenAI: AI processing (see below).
  • Apple: in-app purchases and, if you use it, speech recognition and Sign in with Apple.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscription status. It receives an account identifier (not your email address) and your purchase status.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications and app updates.
  • Resend: sends account emails such as password reset codes.
  • Sentry: receives crash and error reports.
  • SerpAPI: price and product-image lookups use a product name and store, with no account information. Barcode scans are looked up in public product databases (Open Food Facts, UPCItemDB). Recall checks use public U.S. government recall data.

Third-Party AI Services

MajaSam uses OpenAI's API for AI features: reading receipts, checking coverage, assessing damage, drafting claims and disputes, and answering questions about a purchase in the Purchase Assistant. When you use one of these features, the relevant text, receipt image, or question is sent to OpenAI for processing, and the result is shown to you.

OpenAI's API terms state that data submitted via the API is not used to train their models by default. You can review OpenAI's policies at openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies.

Recall alerts also use OpenAI in the background: from time to time our server compares the product names in your vault with public recall notices and sends you an alert if one matches. Only product names are sent for this. We do not use any other AI service for processing your content.

Google API Limited Use Compliance

MajaSam's use of information received from Google APIs, including Gmail data, adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the receipt-scanning feature within MajaSam. The text of receipt emails is sent to OpenAI's API only to extract purchase details for you; it is not used to develop, improve, or train any AI or machine learning model, ours or OpenAI's. Raw or derived Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties other than as described here. No person at MajaSam reads your email content except with your consent, to investigate abuse or a security issue, or to comply with the law.

Data Protection

  • Encryption in transit: all data sent between the app and our servers uses TLS/HTTPS.
  • Encryption at rest: data in our database and file storage (Supabase) is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Records saved on your phone are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with the key kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Access controls: every request to our server needs your signed-in session. You can reach only your own data, plus purchases that a family vault member has chosen to share with you. Receipt and document files are private and are opened through short-lived links.
  • Two-step sign-in: available for every account, with recovery codes in case you lose your phone.
  • Minimal retention of email: email content is processed in memory and not stored after extraction.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers listed above to run the app, with family vault members you approve (only the purchases you choose to share), and when required by law or to protect safety and security.

Family Vault

If you create or join a family vault, the owner approves who joins. Members can see the purchases you choose to share, and you can hide details such as price for each shared item. Members can leave a vault at any time.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your purchases, claims, and files are kept until you delete them or delete your account. When you choose Delete Account in the app, we remove your purchases, claims, family memberships, notification tokens, and uploaded receipts and documents, and then delete your sign-in account. Copies in database backups are removed as the backups expire, within 30 days. Error reports and server logs are kept by our providers for a limited time and do not contain your receipts or passwords.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Edit or delete any purchase, claim, or document in the app.
  • Download a copy of your data from More, Download My Data.
  • Delete your account from More, Delete Account. This cannot be undone.
  • Disconnect Gmail at any time. AI features run only when you open them, so you can choose not to use them. Background recall matching currently applies to every account; to stop it, delete your purchases or your account.
  • If you live where privacy laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act apply, you may have additional rights, including access, correction, deletion, and portability. Email us and we will respond.

Where Your Data Is Processed

Our database is in the United States, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using MajaSam you understand your information may be processed there.

Children's Privacy

MajaSam is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app and update the date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or want to use your rights, contact us at support@majasam.com.
