MajaSam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

MajaSam helps you track purchases, return windows, and warranties and, if you choose, scan Gmail for receipts. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Information We Collect

  • Account: your email address, your name, and your password (stored only as a salted hash by our sign-in provider; we never see it). If you sign in with Apple instead, we receive an Apple identifier, your name (only if you share it, and Apple sends it only the first time), and either your real email or Apple's private relay address. We never receive your Apple ID password
  • Purchases you add or import: product names, prices, stores, dates, warranty and return details, notes, receipt photos, warranty and loss documents, claims and disputes, and the call notes you type after phoning a company (stored in your account only if you turn on cloud backup)
  • Gmail receipts, only if you connect Gmail (see below)
  • Subscription status. Apple handles payment; we never receive your card details
  • A notification token, to send reminders and recall alerts you turn on, and family questions and answers. A token belongs to the phone and to the account signed in on it. Signing in on a phone as someone else, or signing out, removes the earlier account's link to that phone
  • Family questions and answers, if you use "Ask family" (see Sharing and Family Vault)
  • Your shopping country choice for Check before you buy (kept on your phone, and sent with price and recall lookups so they use that country's stores, currency and rules)
  • Technical and crash information that helps us fix problems
  • Voice questions, if you use the microphone: Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text, and we receive only the text
  • Questions you type or say to Ask my stuff or to the assistant on a purchase, and what you describe in Repair or replace and Find ideas. We do not keep the conversation (see AI Processing and Services That Process Your Data)

Gmail Data

If you connect Gmail, MajaSam requests read-only access (gmail.readonly). It is used only to:

  • Find purchase receipts and subscription confirmations (only messages with receipt-style subjects or senders, including receipt PDFs)
  • Extract the product, price, store, and date
  • Add the purchases you choose to your vault

We do not store your emails. A receipt's text is sent to our server only for AI extraction and is not kept after processing. Your Gmail access tokens stay on your device. We require two-step sign-in on your account before Gmail can be connected. You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Settings, and signing out also revokes it.

How We Use Your Information

  • If you turn on cloud backup, store and sync your purchases across your devices
  • Track return windows and warranties and help you file claims
  • Read receipts and answer questions with the AI features you use
  • Send reminders and recall alerts you turn on
  • Keep the service secure and respond to support requests

Cloud Backup (Your Choice)

When you first use MajaSam on a phone, you choose whether to back up your purchases to your account. If you turn backup on, your purchases, claims, disputes, call notes, receipt photos and documents are stored in your account with our database and file-storage provider (Supabase, United States) so you can restore them on a new phone and share items with family. If you leave it off, they stay on your phone only, and signing out or losing the phone erases them. You can change this at any time in More → Cloud backup.

Turning backup off stops new uploads but does not delete what was already backed up; you can delete items individually or delete your account to remove everything. Family sharing needs backup on, because shared items are stored in the cloud.

Receipts you scan and questions you ask are still sent to our AI service to be read, and recall checks send product names and models, whether or not backup is on. The text of Gmail emails is never saved to your backup. When AI has to read a receipt, its text is sent for that purpose only (see Gmail).

Photos, Apple Home and Camera

  • Find Receipts in Photos: if you use it, MajaSam reads your most recent photos on your phone to find pictures of receipts, using Apple's text recognition on your device. Photos are not uploaded or stored by us. Only a photo you choose to import is processed as a receipt, in the same way as any receipt you add.
  • Import from Apple Home: if you choose it, MajaSam reads the list of devices in your Home (each device's name, maker, model and room). It never controls your devices and does not receive serial numbers. The devices you select are added to your vault, and their maker and model names are sent to our recall service to check for safety recalls, like any other purchase.
  • Point and Know and label scanning: these use your camera live and compare what it sees with your own vault on your phone. The camera picture is not recorded or sent anywhere. A barcode number or product name may be looked up in public product databases.
  • Check before you buy and Aisle scan: the camera reads barcodes and printed text on your phone. We do not store the picture. What leaves your phone depends on the product:
    • A barcode number is looked up in public product databases (such as Open Food Facts and UPCitemdb) straight from your phone, so they can see your phone's network address. It is also sent to our server, which searches the web for the number to find the product's name.
    • If no barcode source can name the product, the printed text the camera read (at most 80 lines) is sent to our server and to OpenAI to name it. Before anything is sent, your phone removes lines that look like names and addresses (such as the lines after "Ship to"), street addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, tracking numbers, and serial, device and network identifiers.
    • In Check before you buy only, if the product still cannot be named, the sharp photo the scanner took of the box can be sent to our server and then to OpenAI to identify the product. Our server does not save it, and the photo is deleted from your phone when the check ends. Aisle scan does not send photos.
    • The product's name, brand, model, barcode number and the country you chose to shop in are used to look up recalls, prices and return rules. If you type the store you are in or the price on the tag, they are used for the price and return lookups and, if you choose Ask family, shared as described under Sharing and Family Vault.
    • Text and photos go to OpenAI only if you have allowed AI features. Nothing from these scans is saved in your account unless you tap "I bought it".
  • Claim photos: when you add photos to a claim, MajaSam can read the picture of a serial label on your phone, using Apple's text recognition, to check it against the serial number saved with the purchase. That picture is not uploaded for this check. Photos on a claim are kept like your other claim files (on your phone, and in your account if you turn on cloud backup) and are put in the claim package you create.
  • Siri answers: so Siri can answer "Ask my stuff" without opening the app, MajaSam keeps a short summary of your own purchases on your phone, in the same private storage the widget uses (the summary is not uploaded and does not include other family members' items). Siri says nothing about your purchases while the phone is locked. Fixed questions such as warranties, returns, spending, recalls and receipts are answered from that summary. Other questions are answered by Apple's on-device model on phones that have Apple Intelligence, so nothing is sent to us or to OpenAI; on other phones Siri says it cannot answer them.
  • Sharing a photo or a link to MajaSam: when you share a photo, MajaSam reads its text on your phone to tell a receipt from a product photo, and asks you what it is. If you say it is a product you are thinking of buying, it is named the way Check before you buy names a box (if its text is not enough, the photo may be sent to our server and OpenAI to make a best guess, only if you have allowed AI features, and you are asked to confirm the guess before anything is checked). When you share a shopping link, your phone opens that page directly to read its title, and the product name is then looked up like a name you typed. If a shared link points to a picture, your phone asks that site what it is and, if it is a picture, downloads it and treats it like a photo you shared. If you share several pictures that are pieces of one long receipt, you can join them into one picture; joining happens on your phone, and the joined picture is then treated like any receipt you add. A photo you say is of something you own is saved with that item.
  • Recall requests: Fix my recall prepares a request from your record and the recall notice. MajaSam never sends it. You send it yourself from your own mail app, or you open the maker's website or phone number from the app. The note of when you sent it, and a reminder to follow up, stay on your phone.
  • Compare and Repair or replace: your Compare list is kept on your phone for up to 7 days, and Repair or replace estimates and prices are kept on your phone for a few days to save repeat lookups. They are not uploaded.

Services That Process Your Data

  • Supabase: sign-in and, if you turn on cloud backup, our database and file storage (hosted in the United States)
  • Render: the server that relays your requests
  • OpenAI: AI processing (see below)
  • Apple: in-app purchases, speech recognition, and Sign in with Apple (used only to create and secure your account)
  • RevenueCat: subscription status (it receives an account identifier, not your email)
  • Expo: push notifications and app updates
  • Resend: account emails such as password reset codes
  • Sentry: crash and error reports
  • SerpAPI: price, barcode, product-image and shopping-idea lookups use a product name, brand, barcode number, store, the words you type in Find ideas, and your shopping country, with no account details. Our server keeps the results of an idea search for an hour and shares them with anyone who types the same words; they contain no personal data
  • Public product and recall data: barcodes are looked up in public product databases (Open Food Facts, UPCitemdb) from your phone. Recall checks use public recall lists from the United States (CPSC), the European Union (Safety Gate), France (RappelConso) and the United Kingdom (Food Standards Agency). Our server downloads these lists and compares your product's name, brand, model and barcode with them. Nothing about you is sent to the agencies
  • GitHub Pages: hosts majasam.com and a public list of store and maker contact details that the app downloads every few hours. The download carries no account details or personal data

Third-Party AI Services

We use OpenAI's API to read receipts, check coverage, assess damage, draft claims, estimate repair costs, and answer questions about your purchases. When you use one of these features, the relevant text, image, or question is sent to OpenAI and the result is shown to you. OpenAI's API terms state that API data is not used to train their models by default.

The first time you use an AI feature, the app asks for your permission, and you can turn AI features off at any time in More → Settings → Privacy & Security.

To keep the service available and its cost reasonable, each account has a daily allowance for AI features. Heavy use can pause them until the next day. Everything else in MajaSam keeps working.

Check before you buy and Aisle scan also use OpenAI to name a product from its printed text or, in Check before you buy, from a photo of the box when barcode lookups cannot; to compare a product's name, brand and model with recall notices; and, for shops in the United States, to estimate a typical return window. Your phone first removes names, addresses, contact details and identifiers from printed text, as described under Photos, Apple Home and Camera. If you turn AI features off, none of this is sent.

Ask my stuff answers questions about your own purchases. Questions about warranty and return dates, totals, recalls and receipts are answered on your phone from your saved records, and nothing is sent anywhere. For any other question, on a phone with Apple Intelligence the answer is also made on the phone. Otherwise your question, and the saved details of the few purchases it seems to be about (name, brand, model, store, purchase date, price, warranty and return dates, serial number, whether a receipt is saved, the titles of active recalls, and the status of claims), are sent to OpenAI through our server. Your whole list of purchases is never sent, purchases that belong to other family members are never included, and we do not keep the conversation.

Repair or replace sends the product's name, brand, model and category, the problem you describe, and your currency to OpenAI to estimate a repair cost, a typical lifespan and the price of a new one. What you paid, when you bought it and your other details are not sent. The estimate is kept on your phone for about a week, and our server keeps one answer for each product and problem, so people with the same product and problem share it.

Recall alerts also use OpenAI in the background to compare the product names in your vault with public recall notices. Only product names and brands are sent for this, and it runs only for accounts that have allowed AI features.

We do not use any other AI service for your content.

You can review OpenAI's policies at openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies.

Google API Limited Use Compliance

MajaSam's use of information received from Google APIs, including Gmail data, adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Gmail data is used only to provide the receipt-scanning feature. Receipt text is sent to OpenAI's API only to extract purchase details for you; it is not used to develop, improve, or train any AI or machine learning model. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared except as described here. No person at MajaSam reads your email content except with your consent, to investigate abuse or a security issue, or to comply with the law.

Data Protection

  • Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS
  • Data in our database and file storage is encrypted at rest (AES-256). Records saved on your phone are encrypted, with the key kept in the iOS Keychain
  • Every request to our server needs your signed-in session, and you can reach only your own data and purchases that family members have chosen to share with you
  • Receipt and document files are private and opened through short-lived links
  • Two-step sign-in is available for every account, with recovery codes if you lose your phone
  • For your protection you are signed out after 30 days, or after 7 days without opening the app. Your saved purchases stay on your phone and come back when you sign in

Sharing and Family Vault

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services above to run the app, with family vault members you approve, and when required by law or to protect safety and security.

Family vault members see the purchases you choose to share. They see the product photo, and the receipt, serial number and notes too unless you switch on Hide receipt, serial & notes for that item. Your warranty and other documents, and your disputes and call notes, stay private to you. Members can leave a vault at any time.

If you use Ask family, the question you send contains the product's name, brand and model, the store, the tag price you entered, and the result lines from your check. It goes only to the vault members you pick (everyone in the vault, or the people you tick). Those people can answer Yes or No with an optional short note, and the answers are visible to you and to the other people the question was sent to. A question and its answers disappear for everyone after 7 days, or when you remove the question, and are deleted if you delete your account. Notifications about questions and answers are delivered through Expo's push service and show the person's name and the product name.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your purchases, claims, disputes, call notes, and files are kept on your phone until you delete them. If you turned on cloud backup, copies in your account are kept until you delete them or delete your account, and turning backup off does not remove them. When you choose Delete Account, we remove your purchases, claims, disputes, call notes, family memberships, notification tokens, and uploaded receipts and documents, and then delete your sign-in account. If you signed in with Apple, we also tell Apple to disconnect MajaSam from your Apple ID. We also delete your customer record at RevenueCat, our subscription provider. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; to stop billing, cancel it in iOS Settings under your name, Subscriptions. Copies in database backups are removed as the backups expire, within 30 days. Error reports and server logs are kept by our providers for a limited time. They do not contain your receipts, passwords or sign-in tokens, but they can contain product names and barcodes you look up. A conversation in Ask my stuff or the assistant is not saved; it disappears when you leave the screen.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Edit or delete any purchase, claim, dispute, call note, or document
  • Download a copy of your data from More → Download My Data
  • Delete your account from More → Delete Account (this cannot be undone)
  • Disconnect Gmail at any time
  • Turn AI features off in More → Settings → Privacy & Security. When they are off, nothing is sent to OpenAI and background recall matching skips your account. You can turn them on again at any time

If you live where privacy laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act apply, you may have additional rights, including access, correction, deletion, and portability. Email us at support@majasam.com and we will respond.

Where Your Data Is Processed

Our database is in the United States, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

Children's Privacy

MajaSam is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at support@majasam.com and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app and update the date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or want to use your rights, contact us at support@majasam.com.
