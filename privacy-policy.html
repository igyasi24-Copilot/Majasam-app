MajaSam Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

MajaSam is an iOS app that helps users track purchases, monitor return windows and warranties, and scan Gmail for receipts.

Gmail Data

MajaSam requests read-only access to your Gmail account. This access is used solely to:

  • Scan your inbox for purchase receipts and subscription confirmations
  • Extract product name, price, store name, and purchase date from receipt emails
  • Populate your personal purchase vault within the app

MajaSam does not store your emails. Email content is sent to our server only for AI-based receipt extraction and is not retained after processing. We do not read, store, or share emails unrelated to purchases.

Data We Collect

  • Purchase information extracted from receipts (product name, price, store, date)
  • Gmail OAuth tokens stored securely on your device

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Revoking Access

You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Settings inside the MajaSam app. This revokes all Gmail access immediately.
