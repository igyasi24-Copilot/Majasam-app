Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

1 Is my data stored locally?

Yes — your purchase data lives on your device first. Cloud backup (available on Pro) lets you optionally sync for peace of mind.

2 What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks unlimited purchase tracking, Gmail receipt import, Family Vault sharing, cloud backup, CSV/PDF export, AI damage assessor, and per-item reminder preferences.

3 How does Gmail scanning work?

MajaSam reads only emails that match receipt patterns — order confirmations and shipping notifications from retailers. No other emails are accessed or stored.

4 What is the Family Vault?

Family Vault lets one member create a shared vault. Other family members join with an invite code and their purchases become visible to all members.
