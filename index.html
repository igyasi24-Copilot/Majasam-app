Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

1 How do the safety recall alerts work?

MajaSam checks every purchase you add against the live CPSC recall database (10,000+ records). When a match is found, you get a push notification with the full recall title, the specific hazard, and exactly what to do — including a direct link to the CPSC recall page. Checks run automatically in the background so you don't have to do anything.

2 Is my data stored locally?

Yes — your purchase data is stored on your device and optionally synced to your private account on Supabase (a secure cloud database). Your receipts are never sold or shared. Gmail scanning reads only receipt-pattern emails; no email content is stored on our servers.

3 What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks unlimited purchase tracking, CPSC safety recall monitoring, price match & price drop alerts, Gmail receipt import, Family Vault sharing, cloud backup, CSV/PDF export, AI damage assessor, iOS widget, Live Activity, and per-item reminder preferences.

4 How does Gmail scanning work?

MajaSam reads only emails that match receipt patterns — order confirmations and shipping notifications from retailers. No other emails are accessed or stored. You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Settings.

5 What is the Family Vault?

Family Vault lets one member create a shared vault. Other family members join with an invite code and their purchases become visible to all members — including safety recall alerts across everyone's items.

6 When is MajaSam launching?

MajaSam is coming soon to the iOS App Store. The app is in final pre-launch testing. Follow along at majasam.com for the launch announcement.
